drafts only. nothing submitted. voice: lowercase, no em dashes, spare emoji.

big caveat: the site is an email waitlist right now. product hunt, betalist and most directories are fine with that. show hn is not (hn rules say no landing pages or signup pages), so hold show hn until there's a real feed people can click around.

## 1. product hunt

- name: startupfans
- tagline (47 chars): onlyfans for startups. a curated feed of teams
- alt taglines:
  - exclusive content from early startup teams 😏 (44)
  - follow startups, see what they ship every day (46)
- topics: social networking, startup community, build in public, marketing
- pricing: free

description (under 260 chars):
> startupfans is a curated community of early startups. selected startups get a company feed where anyone on the team can post what they shipped, wins and behind the scenes photos. fans follow the whole company, and we push the best posts out. free to join.

first maker comment:
> hey product hunt 👋 ian here, one of the makers.
>
> every startup has a #wins slack channel full of great stuff nobody outside the company ever sees. what does get shared ends up scattered across a few personal accounts, and company pages on linkedin are mostly dead.
>
> so we built startupfans. basically onlyfans for startups, minus the paywall.
>
> how it works:
> - startups apply and we pick who gets in. it's curated, so the feed stays good
> - anyone on the team can post. a feature shipped, a launch, a photo from the team dinner
> - fans follow the company and get the whole team's feed in one place
> - we push the best posts to our fans, so early teams get seen
>
> it's free for startups and for fans. we're letting the first batch in today.
>
> if you're building something early, apply and tell us what you shipped this week. if you just like watching startups get built, come be a fan.
>
> would love to hear which startup you'd subscribe to first

4. "you've been selected" acceptance card, to sell the curated angle
5. side by side: a dead linkedin company page vs a busy startupfans feed

best launch time: 00:01 pacific (08:01 pt lisbon) on a tuesday, wednesday or thursday. tomorrow is tuesday 13 oct, which works. schedule it tonight. line up the team and the selected startups to leave real comments in the first 2 hours. don't ask for upvotes publicly, ph penalises it.

- pitch: startupfans is a curated community where early startups post what they ship and get promoted to fans. we're picking the first batch now, free for startups and fans.

## 6. five more free launch directories

| site | url | notes |
|---|---|---|
| uneed | https://www.uneed.best/submit-a-tool | daily launches with a leaderboard and weekly newsletter. free queue is long, paid skips it. good maker crowd |
| peerlist launchpad | https://peerlist.io/launchpad | weekly launchpad, needs a peerlist profile (ian's personal). launches run monday to sunday, so submit for this week asap |
| fazier | https://www.fazier.com/submit | daily ph style launches, free option, badges and a newsletter |
| startup fame | https://startupfa.me/submit | free listing with a "featured on" badge requirement for free tier, quick approval |
| better launch | https://www.betterlaunch.co/submit-your-startup | free editorial listing, reviewed within 48h, free launches go out on a monday, standouts go in their weekly email |
