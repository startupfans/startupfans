# investor email campaign (sign up + nominate)

Internal drafts. Nothing sent yet. Companion to [`investor-dms.md`](investor-dms.md) (X). More serious tone than social; no OnlyFans joke in the primary send.

## brief

Cold or warm email to early-stage VCs, scouts, and accelerator community leads. Dual ask: create a StartupFans account so they can follow portfolio companies, then reply with ~3 pre-seed/seed names to onboard this week. Success = account created and/or a shortlist of startups we can invite by hand.

Sender: Ian, first person. Primary link: https://startupfans.co  
If invite-only is still on, admit them from the reply (or send a magic link) before chasing the nomination.

## do not say

- hackathon, launchathon, offsite, Lightdash, "72 hours", "3 days"
- OnlyFans / "onlyfans for startups" in email A or the follow-up (optional one soft line only in email B if the recipient already knows the joke)
- fake traction numbers, founding-spot pricing, paywalls

## email A — short (~120 words)

```
subject: portfolio updates between the monthly emails

Hi {first_name},

{recent_portfolio_or_thesis}

I'm Ian. I built StartupFans: one public feed per startup where anyone on the team can post what they're shipping, what they're working on, and the day-to-day that usually stays in Slack.

It's meant for people who care about early companies between the monthly update emails. Free for founders and for fans.

Two asks, if useful:

1. Create an account and follow a few of your companies: https://startupfans.co
2. Reply with 3 pre-seed or seed names from {firm}. We'll set their pages up this week and you can follow them from day one.

Happy to keep it short either way.

Ian
```

## email B — slightly longer (~180 words)

```
subject: quick ask: 3 startups from {firm}

Hi {first_name},

{recent_portfolio_or_thesis}

I'm Ian. StartupFans is a curated community of early startups. Each company gets one team feed: shipped work, work-in-progress, and the photos and notes that never make it to LinkedIn. Fans follow the company; founders control what goes out.

For investors and scouts, the useful bit is continuity. Monthly updates are fine, but most of the signal sits in Slack and across thirty personal accounts. A company feed gives you a place to watch momentum, and a cleaner link to send after a pitch or demo day.

It's free. No paywall for fans or startups right now.

If this is relevant for {firm}:

1. Sign up at https://startupfans.co and follow the companies you already back.
2. Reply with ~3 pre-seed or seed portfolio names. I'll onboard them this week and make sure you can follow each one.

If you'd rather intro me to a community lead or associate who owns portfolio marketing, that works too.

Ian
```

## send notes

- Prefer personal addresses over firm inboxes. One email per person; don't blast partners and associates at the same firm on the same day unless you know the handoff.
- If they only nominate: onboard the startups, then invite the VC by email so the follow CTA is real.
- If they only sign up: ask once for nominations in a short reply, then stop.
- Keep the X DM list in sync mentally: same people, different channel. Don't double-ping the same day.
